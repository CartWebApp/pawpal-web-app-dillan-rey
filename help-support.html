PawPal — Help & Support

Need assistance? We're here to help — even though PawPal is currently a conceptual, non-commercial prototype, we want your demo experience to be smooth and enjoyable.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is PawPal a real product?
No — PawPal is a fictional wellness tracker concept created for demonstration, educational, or portfolio purposes. It is not active, not for sale, and not medically approved.

Does PawPal monitor my dog in real-time?
No. All features shown (tracking, stats, health insights, etc.) are simulated previews only — they do not connect to real devices or data.

Can I use PawPal for real veterinary or emergency purposes?
Absolutely not. Please consult a licensed veterinarian for any real health or behavioral concerns.
